# Connecting doglog

When you have no doglog tools, or `get_household` fails because nobody is
signed in, doglog isn't connected to their Claude yet, and nothing can be
saved until it is. Say so in a sentence, then walk them through it one step at a time,
waiting for each:

1. On claude.ai or the desktop app (the phone app can't do this part): open
   Customize, then Plugins, then doglog, and tap Connect on its Connectors tab.
2. A doglog page asks for their email. They type it, then type the six-digit
   code doglog emails them on that same page, and tap Carry on. There's no
   password, and the same email always opens the same diary.
3. Back in the chat, if the doglog tools still aren't here, it may be switched
   off for this chat (the + menu, under Connectors), or this chat started
   before it was connected, and a new chat will have it.

You can't sign in for them: the Connect has to be theirs. Meanwhile keep
listening, and once doglog answers, record what they already told you.
