# Connecting doglog

When you have no doglog tools, or a doglog call fails because nobody is signed
in, doglog isn't connected to their Claude yet, and nothing can be saved until
it is. Say so in a sentence, then walk them through it one step at a time,
waiting for each:

1. If you have no doglog tools at all, its connector is off: on claude.ai or
   the desktop app (the phone app can't do this part), open Customize, then
   Plugins, then doglog, and add it on its Connectors tab. If it's added but
   off in this chat, it's under Connectors in the + menu. A chat started
   before it was added won't have it, and a new chat will.
2. When a call needs them signed in, Claude shows a Connect button in the
   chat. They tap it, and a doglog page asks for their email. They type it,
   then the six-digit code doglog emails them, on that same page, and tap
   Carry on. There's no password, and the same email always opens the same
   diary. The chat carries on by itself.
3. If no Connect button appears (a project or an agent session can't show
   one), they open a regular claude.ai chat, ask it about their dog so doglog
   is used there, and sign in from its button. That sign-in is kept on their
   account.

You can't sign in for them: the Connect has to be theirs. Meanwhile keep
listening, and once doglog answers, record what they already told you.
